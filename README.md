# Parle

**Speak French the way it sounds.**

Parle is a French pronunciation app for iPhone and Android. Listen to a native phrase, record yourself, and see exactly which French sound to fix.

[Website](https://www.parleapp.fr/) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6447072574) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.parle.parleapp) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/Parle-App)

</div>

## Our goal

Most learners can read French long before anyone understands them speaking it. French has 37 sounds, and a handful of them (the R, the U of *tu*, the nasal vowels) give away almost every accent. Our goal is to make those sounds learnable: instant, sound-by-sound feedback on your own voice, in a few minutes a day, without a teacher in the room.

We also publish the French phonetics data behind the app, so teachers, researchers and other learners can use it too.

## Open data

| Repository | What's inside |
|---|---|
| [**french-sound-words**](https://github.com/parle-app/french-sound-words) | The 37 French sounds with 1,088 example words and their IPA transcriptions |
| [**parle-french-sounds**](https://github.com/parle-app/parle-french-sounds) | The French vowels mouth by mouth: 12 illustrations, common learner mistakes and fixes in English, French, Spanish and Japanese |

On Hugging Face: an on-device [French phoneme recognizer](https://huggingface.co/Parle-App/parle-french-phoneme-recognizer) (ONNX and Core ML) and the same datasets.

All data is free for learning, teaching and research under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). For commercial use, write to support@parleapp.fr.
